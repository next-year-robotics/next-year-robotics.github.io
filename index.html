Why next year?

“Robotics will be solved next year.”

We have heard it every year since 2016. Every year something real happens, and every year the answer moves one page.

  1. 2016AlphaGo beats Lee Sedol.Next year
  2. 2017“Attention Is All You Need” introduces the Transformer.Next year
  3. 2018OpenAI’s Dactyl hand learns dexterity entirely in simulation.Next year
  4. 2019Dactyl solves a Rubik’s Cube with one hand.Next year
  5. 2020GPT-3.Next year
  6. 2021Boston Dynamics’ Atlas runs a parkour course.Next year
  7. 2022ChatGPT. SayCan and RT-1 bring language and transformers to robots.Next year
  8. 2023ALOHA and ACT, Diffusion Policy, and RT-2, the first VLA.Next year
  9. 2024Mobile ALOHA, OpenVLA, π0.Next year
  10. 2025Gemini Robotics, GR00T N1, Helix, π0.5.Next year
  11. 2026Next Year Robotics starts building Maknae.Next year.
    For real this time.

So we named ourselves after that year.

We build open robots end to end: frame CAD, boards and firmware, ROS 2, and the apps people use around the robot. Everything we make is on GitHub.

Now building

Maknae

Mobile Assistant for Kin’s Needs And Errands

A small mobile robot for older adults who have trouble getting around. It brings things like a water bottle or glasses at home, walks along with the person outside, and lets family know when something goes wrong.

  • mobile-robotThe robot: frame CAD, base interface board, STM32 firmware, and ROS 2 on a Raspberry Pi 5↗
  • senior-care-androidAndroid app that talks to the robot: destinations, trips, and SOS with the phone’s location↗
  • senior-care-dashboardCaregiver web dashboard for robot status, care events and SOS, with push notifications↗
  • mobile-robot-boothExhibition booth dashboard: live camera, digital twin, system status and a talking avatar↗
